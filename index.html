Abstract

We propose PatchScene, a novel diffusion-based framework for large-scale LiDAR scene completion. Unlike existing methods that rely on global latent representations or dense voxel grids, PatchScene adopts a patch-based voxel diffusion paradigm that explicitly generates fine-grained geometry within localized 3D regions. To ensure coherent reconstruction at both spatial and temporal scales, we introduce a confidence-guided spatio-temporal fusion mechanism that integrates overlapping patches and adjacent frames in a unified generative process. Furthermore, we design an Annular-Flow diffusion strategy that leverages the radial density pattern of LiDAR scans to progressively propagate high-fidelity information from near-range to far-range regions, enabling spatially unbounded scene completion. Extensive experiments on the SemanticKITTI benchmark demonstrate that PatchScene achieves state-of-the-art performance across all standard metrics, surpassing previous approaches in both geometric accuracy and temporal consistency. Remarkably, the model trained on 20 m LiDAR ranges generalizes effectively to 50 m scenes without retraining, highlighting its strong scalability and generalization capability for real-world autonomous driving applications.

Method

PatchScene follows a divide-and-conquer generation pipeline in explicit voxel space: local patch completion, confidence-guided fusion, and annular outward diffusion.

1. Voxelize Convert sparse LiDAR into explicit occupancy patches.
2. Denoise Run local diffusion to recover fine-grained geometry.
3. Fuse Merge overlapping spatial patches and adjacent frames.
4. Expand Propagate reliable near-range structure outward.

Patch-Based Voxel Diffusion

The scene is partitioned into overlapping local voxel patches, allowing high-resolution diffusion denoising without the memory cost of a dense global grid.

Spatio-Temporal Fusion

Predictions from overlapping patches and adjacent frames are fused with confidence guidance, improving global smoothness and multi-frame consistency.

Annular-Flow Completion

Generation proceeds from dense near-range regions toward sparse far-range rings, propagating reliable geometry outward for large-scale scene extension.

PatchScene achieves state-of-the-art SemanticKITTI performance across geometric accuracy, distribution fidelity, and voxel-level completeness.

Proprietary Dataset

The supplementary material introduces a high-fidelity real-world LiDAR dataset with 550 clips and over 40 scene categories, covering diverse road topologies, environmental variations, and safety-critical corner cases.

On this dataset, PatchScene reports CD = 0.199 and Voxel IoU = 0.595 at 0.5 m, showing strong robustness under complex real-world driving scenarios.
